## When not to use it
Don't treat the change control notice as the contractual instruction or notice. It's an approval document, and the contract's own instruction and notice process still applies, on time.

It's also the wrong tool when the client says the work isn't a change at all. That's a question of entitlement, and it needs the contract, the records and advice, not a tidy form. AI drafts and organises. The project manager, QS or contract administrator makes the call.

## Common mistakes
- Starting the work before the change is approved, then finding nobody will pay for it.
- Letting the tool pick the clause. Numbers differ between JCT forms and editions, so check the contractual basis against your contract.
- Describing the change vaguely instead of naming the drawings, specification sections and locations affected.
- Guessing the cost and time to get the form out. Build up the cost and check the programme, or say clearly that it's a budget figure.
- Not keeping a change log, so changes approved in principle never become instructions and get missed at final account.

## FAQs
### What is a change control notice in construction?
A form that records a proposed change, why it's needed, what it will cost and what it does to the programme, so the right people can approve or reject it before work starts. It also gives the project a clear audit trail of every change.

### Is a change control notice the same as a variation instruction?
No. The variation instruction, called a Change under JCT Design and Build or a change to the Scope under NEC4, is the contractual step that gives entitlement to payment. The change control notice records the approval. Check your contract and take advice on who can instruct changes and how.

### Who should approve a change control notice?
Whoever holds authority for that level of cost and risk, usually the project manager and commercial manager, then the client's representative. Agree the approval limits at the start of the project, and keep the signed copy with the change log.

### Should work start before a change is approved?
Ideally not. If it has to, for safety or programme reasons, get a written instruction from the person entitled to give it and record the cost as you go. Check your contract and take advice on what notice you need to give.

### Is design development a change under JCT Design and Build?
Usually not. The contractor is responsible for developing its design to meet the Employer's Requirements, so normal development is at its own risk. It becomes a Change when the Employer alters the requirements. Check your contract and take advice, because amendments often move this line.
