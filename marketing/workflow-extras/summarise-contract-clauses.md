## When not to use it
Don't rely on it to decide whether to sign. Heavy amendments, or risky terms on liability, termination or design responsibility, need a solicitor or contracts specialist before signature, not a plain-English briefing.

It's also the wrong tool once you're in a dispute. By then the exact wording matters more than the gist. AI drafts and organises the summary. The QS, contracts manager or legal adviser makes the call.

## Common mistakes
- Giving the tool only the contract form and a few dates. It will summarise the standard form from memory, not your contract. Give it the actual clauses, the Contract Particulars or Contract Data, and the amendments.
- Skimming the amendments. A JCT schedule of amendments or NEC4 Z clauses can change the risk completely, and that's where the traps usually sit.
- Trusting clause numbers in the output. Check every reference against the signed contract.
- Ignoring documents the contract brings in, such as the Employer's Requirements or the Scope, which often carry obligations of their own.
- Pasting the whole contract into a personal AI account. Use a business account with data controls switched on.

## FAQs
### What are the key clauses to check in a construction contract?
Start with the ones that move money and time: payment, change, extension of time and damages for late completion. Then termination, liability caps, insurance and dispute resolution. Check your contract and take advice on anything onerous before you sign.

### What is the difference between JCT and NEC4?
JCT is the more traditional style, with a Contract Administrator issuing instructions and certificates. NEC4 is written in plain language and built around active management: early warnings, a regularly updated programme and compensation events. Both are widely used in the UK, and the client usually chooses.

### What are Z clauses in NEC4?
They're additional conditions the parties add to the standard NEC4 wording. Some are minor tidy-ups. Others move a lot of risk, for example on design liability or time limits. Check your contract and take advice where they change what you're entitled to.

### What are liquidated damages in construction?
A fixed rate, usually per week, the employer can recover if the works finish late without an extension of time. NEC4 calls them delay damages, and they only apply if that option is chosen. Check your contract and take advice before deducting or challenging them, because the notices matter.

### Can AI review a construction contract?
It can set out what the clauses you give it say and where the risks sit. It can't tell you how an adjudicator would read them, and it can miss an amendment. Use it to brief the team, and have someone qualified check anything that matters.
