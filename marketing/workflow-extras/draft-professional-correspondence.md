## When not to use it
Don't rely on it to serve a contractual notice. It can draft the wording, but it doesn't know who your contract says notices go to, how they must be sent or when the clock started. An email that misses those requirements may not count. Check your contract and take advice.

It's also the wrong tool when the relationship has broken down, or the letter is heading towards a claim, a dispute or termination. Have those reviewed by your commercial lead or a solicitor before they go. Once it's sent, it's part of the record.

## Common mistakes
- Letting the draft add facts, dates or clause references you never gave it. Check every one.
- Burying a notice inside a general update email. Send it on its own with a clear subject line. NEC4 expects notifications to be communicated separately from other communications.
- Keeping the apologies the tool adds. AI drafts tend to soften things and say sorry, which can read as accepting responsibility for something that isn't yours.
- Sending from a personal inbox or outside the project's agreed system, so the correspondence record has gaps.

## FAQs
### How do I write a formal letter in construction?
Use a subject line that names the project and the issue, then set out the facts, the impact and what you need, by when. Reference the documents you rely on. Write it as if an adjudicator will read it later, because one might.

### Does an email count as a contractual notice?
It depends on the contract. Many contracts allow email if it goes to the right person in the agreed way, but some notices must be given by a stated method or to a stated address, and NEC4 uses the communication system set out in the Scope. Check your contract and take advice before relying on an email for anything with a time limit.

### What's the difference between an early warning and a compensation event?
Under NEC4, an early warning flags something that could affect cost, time or how the finished works perform, so it can be discussed and reduced. A compensation event notification is about entitlement to more time or money, and it has its own time limits. Send them separately, and if in doubt, send both. Check your contract and take advice.

### Is it safe to use AI for project emails?
Use your company's business account with data controls switched on, such as Copilot inside your Microsoft 365, not a personal account. Leave out anything confidential you're not allowed to share. The sender is still responsible for every word.
