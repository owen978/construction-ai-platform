## When not to use it
Don't write it before the records are final. If the final account isn't agreed or defects are still open, mark those figures as provisional, not settled.

It also doesn't replace the handover documents themselves, such as the CDM 2015 health and safety file and the O&M manuals. And AI can't decide whether practical completion has been reached. That's for whoever certifies it under your contract, such as the contract administrator or the Project Manager.

## Common mistakes
- Writing it from memory weeks after handover instead of from the records.
- Sending the internal version to the client. Margin figures belong in the company archive, not the handover pack. Produce two versions.
- Listing outstanding actions with no owner or date.
- Using terms from the wrong contract. JCT talks about a rectification period, NEC4 about a defects date and defect correction period. Use the words your contract uses.
- Describing extensions of time differently from what was agreed. If something is still being negotiated, say so.

## FAQs
### What should a project closeout report include?
Programme, cost, quality, safety, client feedback, lessons and outstanding actions. The section most often skimped is the outstanding actions: defects, retention, warranties and documents still to hand over. That's the part someone will need in a year's time.

### What is the difference between practical completion and project closeout?
Practical completion (Completion under NEC4) is a contractual milestone, certified under the contract, that usually triggers things like a retention release and the start of the defects period. Closeout is the wider job of finishing everything off: final account, defects, documents and the internal review. Check your contract and take advice on what completion triggers on your job.

### What documents are handed over at the end of a construction project?
Typically the O&M manuals, as-built drawings, test and commissioning certificates, warranties and guarantees, the building regulations completion certificate and the CDM health and safety file. Higher-risk buildings under the Building Safety Act have extra information requirements. Your contract sets out exactly what's needed.

### When is retention released after practical completion?
On many JCT and NEC4 contracts, part is released at completion and the rest at the end of the rectification or defects period, once defects are made good. The percentages and timings depend on your contract, and some contracts have no retention at all. Check your contract and take advice.

### Who should sign off a project closeout report?
The project manager usually writes it, but the commercial lead should confirm the cost figures, the health and safety lead the safety data, and a director should sign off the final version. AI can draft it from your data. It can't confirm the figures are right.
