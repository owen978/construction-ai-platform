## When not to use it
Don't use the summary as your review before signing. It tells the team what the sub-contract says, not whether to accept it. Onerous terms, unusual indemnities or heavy amendments need a proper review and, where the risk is real, a solicitor.

It's also not a back-to-back check on its own. Given only the name of the main contract form, it can't spot the real gaps. AI drafts and organises. The contracts manager, QS or legal adviser makes the call.

## Common mistakes
- Leaving out the amendments. On sub-contracts, that's where most of the risk sits.
- Not lining up notice periods. If the sub-contract allows longer to notify than the main contract does, the main contractor can't pass claims up in time.
- Missing that delay liability is often unliquidated: the subcontractor can be liable for the main contractor's actual losses, not a fixed weekly rate.
- Skipping the set-off and contra charge clauses, which decide what the subcontractor actually gets paid.
- Briefing the site team from a draft instead of the signed version.

## FAQs
### What does back to back mean in a subcontract?
The sub-contract passes the relevant main contract obligations down, so the subcontractor carries the same risks for its package. It's rarely perfect, and gaps in notice periods or design responsibility leave someone exposed. Check your contract and take advice where the gaps matter.

### What should a subcontractor check before signing a subcontract?
Scope and exclusions, design responsibility, payment and notice dates, retention, how variations are valued, delay liability, set-off, indemnities and termination. Read every amendment to the standard form. Check your contract and take advice on anything onerous before you sign.

### Are pay when paid clauses legal in the UK?
Under the Construction Act they're generally ineffective, except where a party further up the chain is insolvent. The amended Act also stops payment being tied to certificates under another contract. If you find one, check your contract and take advice.

### What is a pay less notice?
A notice the payer gives if it intends to pay less than the sum stated as due, with the amount it will pay and why. It must go in before the final date for payment, by the deadline the contract sets. Miss it and the payer will usually have to pay the full notified sum. Check your contract and take advice on the timings.

### Can AI summarise a subcontract?
Yes, if you give it the actual terms, including the amendments, and use a business account with data controls switched on. It will turn them into a plain-English briefing for the site team. It can't judge whether a clause is acceptable.
