## When not to use it
Don't use it to settle numbers that are still in dispute. The summary should show disputed items clearly as disputed, not fold them into an agreed total.

It also won't replace the detailed final account build-up. It summarises an account you've already built; it doesn't create one from scratch.

## Common mistakes
- Mixing agreed and unagreed variations in one total, so nobody can see what's actually settled.
- Losing track of omissions and provisional sum adjustments, which often matter as much as the additions.
- Forgetting contra charges, retention and previous payments when working out the balance.
- Not cross-checking the summary against the last valuation, so the two don't reconcile.
- Sending the account without the records behind each variation.

## FAQs
### What should a final account summary include?
The original contract sum, adjustments for provisional sums and omissions, agreed variations, any unagreed items shown separately, contra charges, retention and previous payments, and the balance due.

### Can AI agree the final account for me?
No. It can structure the summary and check the arithmetic. Agreeing the account is a negotiation based on your records and the contract.

### How do I show items that are still disputed?
List them separately with your value and the other side's value if you know it, so the agreed balance and the disputed amount are both clear.

### When should the final account be issued?
Your contract sets the timing. Check it, because some forms have strict periods for submitting documents and responding.
