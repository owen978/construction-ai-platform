## Overview
AI helps quantity surveyors most with the repetitive written work around valuations, variations, cost plans and reporting. The workflows on this page are ready-to-use prompts for that work: a preliminary cost plan, an NRM2 bill section, an interim valuation, a variation assessment, a subcontractor quote comparison, a tender return analysis, a contract clause summary and a final account summary.

Each one asks for the inputs that matter (your quantities, rates and contract) and turns them into a tidy first draft. AI can't measure reliably from drawings, doesn't know your contract rates and can't decide entitlement. Those stay with you.

Start with one task you do every month, such as the valuation. Run it in a business AI account with data controls, never a personal one, and check every figure against your own spreadsheet. For the wider picture, the longer guide to AI for quantity surveyors is linked further down this page.

## Where AI helps most
- Interim valuations: AI lays out the valuation with cumulative values, retention and previous payments, and writes the covering summary from your figures.
- Variations: it turns an instruction and your build-up into a structured assessment of cost, time and knock-on effects.
- Subcontractor quotes: it puts quotes side by side and pulls out the exclusions, qualifications and scope gaps that are easy to miss.
- Tender returns: it organises the returns into a consistent comparison and drafts the recommendation report for you to finish.
- Cost plans and bills: it builds the structure of an NRM1 elemental cost plan or an NRM2 bill section, ready for your quantities and rates.
- Final accounts: it pulls the contract sum, variations, claims and adjustments into one summary and flags what's still to agree.

## Where to be careful
- Arithmetic. AI can make up figures and get totals wrong. Let the spreadsheet do the maths and use AI for the words around it.
- Rates. Never let the tool invent rates. Use contract rates, rates based on them or properly built-up rates.
- Entitlement and payment notices. Whether a change is a variation, and what a payment or pay less notice must say under the Construction Act, depends on your contract and the facts. Check your contract and take advice.
- Client and cost data. Tender prices, rates and contracts are confidential. Use only a business account your company has approved, and check any confidentiality terms in the contract.

## FAQs
### Will AI replace quantity surveyors?
Not the judgement. AI is taking over a lot of the drafting, summarising and first-pass checking that fills a QS's week. Valuing, negotiating, deciding entitlement and signing off the numbers still need someone who understands the contract and carries the responsibility.

### Can ChatGPT read a bill of quantities?
It can read a bill in Excel or a text-based PDF, summarise it and help you query items. It struggles with scanned PDFs and complicated tables, and it can misread or drop lines. Check what it tells you against the bill, and do the totals in a spreadsheet.

### Can AI value a variation?
It can structure the assessment and write it up, but it can't decide the value. Give it the instruction, your quantities and the contract rates, then check the result against the contract's valuation rules. If entitlement is in doubt, check your contract and take advice.

### Does RICS allow surveyors to use AI?
Yes, with care. RICS has published a professional standard on the responsible use of AI in surveying practice, which expects firms to manage the risks, be clear with clients about how AI is used and apply professional judgement to what it produces. If you're RICS regulated, read it and make sure your use of AI fits.
