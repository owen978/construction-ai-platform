## Post 1: Value / listicle (attach golden-thread-construction-explained-listicle.png)

The golden thread is not a piece of software. It is not a folder on the server either.

It is the record that proves a higher-risk building was designed and built safely, kept digitally, as one version of the truth, and handed over intact.

If you are working on a residential block of 18 metres or 7 storeys and above, this is what it needs to hold:

→ Fire statement and fire strategy
→ Approved plans and specifications
→ The change control log, every change
→ Product data with the fire test evidence
→ Fire stopping photos, recorded by location
→ As-built information for Gateway 3

The two that catch people out are product substitutions and fire stopping. Both disappear once the walls are closed up. If they were not logged and photographed at the time, you cannot prove them later.

Set the thread up before Gateway 2, not in the panic before Gateway 3.

## Post 2: Hook / time-saver (attach golden-thread-construction-explained-hook.png)

Week 22 on a nine-storey residential block in Leeds. The cladding sub reports a 14-week lead time on the specified cavity barrier and offers an "equivalent".

Version one: the site team accepts it by email to protect the programme. Nobody logs it. At Gateway 3 the spec names one product, the photos show another, and nobody can find the test evidence. The completion certificate stalls.

Version two: a change request is raised the same day. The principal designer categorises it against the change control plan. The fire engineer checks the test evidence. The registers are updated and the install is photographed by location.

Two or three hours of effort at the time. Potentially weeks saved at the end.

The boring part is the cross-checking, and that is where AI helps. Export the change log, drawing register and spec index, and ask it to list every document still naming the old product. On this job it found four, including a fire strategy appendix everyone had missed.

People made the decisions. AI did the paperwork.
