## Post 1: Value / listicle (attach chatgpt-prompts-for-construction-listicle.png)

Most people try ChatGPT once on site work, get a generic American document back, and give up.

The tool is not the problem. The prompt is.

A good construction prompt has four parts:

→ A role ("You are a UK site manager working under CDM 2015")
→ The real project facts
→ A fixed format (your headings, your table columns)
→ A guardrail: "Do not invent anything. Write [CONFIRM] where information is missing."

That last line is the one nobody uses. It turns AI from a confident guesser into an assistant that shows you exactly where it needs you.

The six prompts I would give every site team first:

→ Daily site report from phone notes
→ Meeting minutes with an action table
→ Method statement first draft
→ COSHH assessment from a safety data sheet
→ Subcontract tenders compared like for like
→ Variation assessment the same day

AI drafts. A competent person checks and signs off. That part does not change.

## Post 2: Hook / time-saver (attach chatgpt-prompts-for-construction-hook.png)

16:40 on a Tuesday. 24-unit housing scheme in Leicester.

The site manager types her day into her phone:

"Rain till 10. Groundworkers 5 men, stood till 10. Brickies 8 on plots 7 to 10, scaffold on plot 9 not handed over. 12 pallets bricks in, 2 broken. Building control passed plot 4 DPC. Client PM asked for extra window on plot 12."

One prompt later she has a full daily report.

The weather delay recorded with times. The scaffold issue logged as a delay with its cause. The client's verbal instruction kept word for word, ready for the QS.

And where she had not given hours or plant, the report said [CONFIRM] instead of making numbers up.

6 minutes instead of 30. Around 120 hours back over a 60-week job.
