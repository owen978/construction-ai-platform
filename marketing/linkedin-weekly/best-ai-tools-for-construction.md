## Post 1: Value / listicle (attach best-ai-tools-for-construction-listicle.png)

"Which AI tool should we buy?" is the wrong question for most contractors.

The better question is "which job are we trying to speed up?"

Here is how the common jobs actually map:

→ Daily site reports: ChatGPT or Copilot
→ Subcontract and contract review: Claude
→ Levelling five tender returns: Claude
→ Minutes from a Teams meeting: Copilot
→ Takeoff: Bluebeam, then AI to structure the bill
→ Finding the latest drawing or RFI: Procore or ACC, if you already run on them

Most construction admin is reading and writing text. A general assistant at around £20 a month per person covers most of it.

Specialist platforms earn their place only when they can see data you already hold in them.

And none of them sign anything off. The competent person under CDM 2015 is still a person.

## Post 2: Hook / time-saver (attach best-ai-tools-for-construction-hook.png)

A 45 person main contractor in the East Midlands spent under £500 a month on AI.

No new construction platform. No big rollout. Here is what they did:

1. Claude seats for the 8 people in the commercial team (long contracts and tenders)
2. ChatGPT seats for 14 site and project managers (short drafting jobs)
3. Copilot for just 3 people who live in Outlook and Teams
4. Kept Bluebeam for the estimators

Three months later:

Daily site report: 45 minutes down to 10
Subcontract amendment review: half a day down to about an hour
Levelling five tender returns: two days down to half a day

The judgement did not move. Same people deciding what to accept, price and challenge. The reading and drafting is what got faster.
