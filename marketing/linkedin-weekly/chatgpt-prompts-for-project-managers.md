## Post 1: Value / listicle (attach chatgpt-prompts-for-project-managers-listicle.png)

Ask ChatGPT to "write a risk register for a school extension" and you get twenty generic rows with made-up scores and no owners.

It looks finished. Nobody on your project would recognise it.

The fix is not a better tool. It is a better prompt.

Here are 6 jobs AI does well for construction PMs, when you give it the real facts:

→ Draft the risk register (cause, event, effect)
→ Summarise the 3-week look-ahead
→ Write the monthly progress report
→ Turn rough notes into formal minutes
→ Draft NEC4 early warning notices
→ Build the handover checklist

Every prompt that works has the same five parts: a PM role, your real project facts, the contract form, a fixed format, and one line that says "write [CONFIRM] where anything is missing".

That last line is the one most people leave out. It stops the AI guessing dates and clause numbers.

All 22 copy-paste prompts and a UK worked example are linked in the comments.

## Post 2: Hook / time-saver (attach chatgpt-prompts-for-project-managers-hook.png)

Most construction PMs lose 60 to 90 minutes a month formatting the progress report.

Here is a different way, worked through on an example £6.2m school extension (NEC4 Option A):

1. Eight lines of rough notes from the PM
2. One structured prompt
3. A full monthly report back: exec summary, programme, H&S, risks, client decisions

Time from notes to a report ready for a final read: about 8 minutes.

The best bit? Where the notes did not give the compensation event references, the AI wrote "[CONFIRM CE references]" instead of inventing them.

That is the behaviour you want. AI drafts, the PM checks and decides.
