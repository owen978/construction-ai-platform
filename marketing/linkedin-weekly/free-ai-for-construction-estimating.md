## Post 1: Value / listicle (attach free-ai-for-construction-estimating-listicle.png)

You do not need to pay for AI to save hours on your next tender.

The free versions of ChatGPT and Claude already do the slow, low-judgement part of estimating well:

→ Summarise a 200-page tender pack in a minute
→ Turn scope gaps into a numbered list of tender queries
→ Build the bill of quantities skeleton (descriptions and units, quantities left blank)
→ Put three subcontract quotes side by side, exclusions and all
→ Sense-check the finished estimate for missing items

What free AI should never do: give you a rate. It does not know your costs, your suppliers or the Leeds market this month. Any number it invents is a placeholder at best.

Reading and structuring goes to the AI. Pricing and judgement stay with the estimator.

Step-by-step, with the exact prompts, is linked in the comments.

## Post 2: Hook / time-saver (attach free-ai-for-construction-estimating-hook.png)

A small contractor in Leeds priced a 24 m² rear extension using free AI and Excel.

Software cost: nothing.

1. Claude (free) summarised the spec and flagged a contractor-designed warm roof
2. ChatGPT (free) turned the drawings notes into 9 tender queries
3. ChatGPT built the bill structure, the estimator filled in the quantities
4. Excel did the pricing. No AI rates anywhere.
5. Claude compared three roofing quotes and caught the cheapest one excluding the rooflight upstands
6. A final sense check flagged the missing party wall fees

About three hours saved on a one-day pricing job.

One rule before you try it: switch off model training in the settings and strip the client's name and address first.
