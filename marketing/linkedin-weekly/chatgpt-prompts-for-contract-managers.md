## Post 2: Hook / time-saver (attach chatgpt-prompts-for-contract-managers-hook.png)

Eight weeks goes fast on a live job.

Under NEC4 clause 61.3, if the contractor does not notify a compensation event within eight weeks of becoming aware of it, the right to more time or money can be gone.

Here is a 20-minute weekly habit that stops that happening:

1. Paste the week's site diaries and minutes into Claude or ChatGPT
2. Paste the clause 60.1 text from your contract
3. Ask for a register: event, date aware, possible clause, deadline, notice sent
4. Anything within two weeks of its deadline gets flagged URGENT

The worked example in the guide is an uncharted water main strike that was in the diary but never notified. The register catches it with two weeks to spare.
