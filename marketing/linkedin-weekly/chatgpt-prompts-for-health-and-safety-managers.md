## Post 2: Hook / time-saver (attach chatgpt-prompts-for-health-and-safety-managers-hook.png)

A roofing subcontractor sends over a four-page RAMS for composite roof panels.

Reviewing it properly takes about 45 minutes.

With one structured AI prompt, the first pass took under 10. And it caught:

1. Panels lifted before edge protection was signed off
2. Harnesses listed, but no rescue plan for a suspended worker
3. Crane lifts with no lift plan or appointed person
4. No wind speed limit for handling panels

The missing rescue plan is one of the most common serious gaps in work at height RAMS.

The H&S manager still checked every point against the site and adjusted two of them. That is the right split.
