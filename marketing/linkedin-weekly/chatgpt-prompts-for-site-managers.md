## Post 1: Value / listicle (attach chatgpt-prompts-for-site-managers-listicle.png)

Most site managers write the day's paperwork at 7pm in the cabin, from memory, when they would rather be going home.

That is when records get thin. And thin records lose disputes.

Here are 6 site jobs AI can draft in minutes, if you give it the real facts:

→ Daily site report from your phone notes
→ Toolbox talk on today's actual hazard
→ RAMS review before a trade starts
→ Snag list from your walkround notes
→ 3-week look-ahead for each trade
→ A firm, fair email to a trade that is behind

The one rule that matters on site: tell the AI "use only my notes, write [CONFIRM] where anything is missing".

A site record with an invented detail in it is worse than no record at all. That line keeps it honest.

## Post 2: Hook / time-saver (attach chatgpt-prompts-for-site-managers-hook.png)

Eight lines of phone notes. One prompt. A full daily site report in about 5 minutes.

We tested it on an example 42-apartment timber frame job in Leeds:

1. Notes typed through the day: weather, labour, a rejected panel delivery, a near miss
2. Pasted into AI with a fixed set of report headings
3. Back came a structured report, groundworks shortfall flagged against programme

The best bit was the verbal instruction from the client's rep to move a kitchen window.

The AI recorded the facts, asked for the rep's name, and did not decide whether it was a variation. That call stays with the site manager and the QS.

25 to 40 minutes a day becomes 5. Over a week, that is two hours back on site.
