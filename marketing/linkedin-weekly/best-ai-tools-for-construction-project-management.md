## Post 1: Value / listicle (attach best-ai-tools-for-construction-project-management-listicle.png)

Most construction project managers do not need a new platform to get value from AI.

The hours go on writing, not deciding. Reports, minutes, risk registers, change notices.

That is exactly where a general AI assistant earns its keep. Here is what we saw on a £9.4m school extension under JCT D&B:

→ Progress meeting minutes issued the same afternoon, not 5 days later
→ Monthly client report drafted in 3 hours instead of 2 days
→ Risk register reviewed fortnightly in 30 minutes
→ Change control notices drafted in 20 minutes from the instruction and the clause
→ Programme narratives in plain English in about an hour

The PM still decided what to report, what to escalate and what counted as a variation. AI just took the drafting off them.

If you are on Microsoft 365, start with Copilot. Add Claude or ChatGPT for contracts and reports. Only switch on Procore or ACC AI if your projects already run there.

## Post 2: Hook / time-saver (attach best-ai-tools-for-construction-project-management-hook.png)

The monthly client report used to take our PM the best part of two days.

Now the first draft takes about three hours, including checking.

Nothing clever. Just a repeatable routine:

1. Paste in last month's report, the site diary notes and the cost report
2. Add the programme export and this month's changes
3. Ask AI for a first draft in the client's format
4. Read every line, correct it, add the judgement calls

The decisions stayed human. The typing did not.
