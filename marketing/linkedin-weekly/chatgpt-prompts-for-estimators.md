## Post 1: Value / listicle (attach chatgpt-prompts-for-estimators-listicle.png)

Most of an estimator's tender period is not pricing. It is reading.

200 drawings, a 90-page spec, a contract with amendments, all due back in three weeks. The pricing judgement gets squeezed into the last few days.

Here are 6 tender jobs AI can take off your desk, if you give it the real documents:

→ Summarise the tender pack into one page
→ Find drawings that are missing or out of date
→ Draft the tender query list
→ Level subcontractor quotes against your scope
→ Build the preliminaries checklist
→ Write your tender qualifications

The one rule that matters: tell the AI "do not suggest rates or quantities, write [CHECK] where the documents are unclear".

A made-up rate in a live bid becomes a loss on site. The numbers stay yours.

All 20 copy-paste prompts and a worked UK example are linked in the comments.

## Post 2: Hook / time-saver (attach chatgpt-prompts-for-estimators-hook.png)

Three groundworks quotes. The cheapest was £27,000 below the next one.

It wasn't the cheapest.

We ran an example primary school extension tender in Nottinghamshire through one quote-levelling prompt:

1. Our own scope table for groundworks, 14 items
2. Three subcontractor quotes pasted in
3. Back came an included / excluded / not stated grid for every item

Quote A had left out the piling mat, contaminated muck away and traffic management, and had no allowance for term-time working hours.

The AI flagged every gap as [PRICE NEEDED] and did not guess a single number. Once the estimator priced the gaps, A was no longer in the running.

Two to three hours of levelling became about 25 minutes.
