## Post 1: Value / listicle (attach ai-use-cases-in-construction-listicle.png)

When people talk about AI in construction, they picture robots and drones.

The real wins right now are far more boring. And far more useful.

They are the documents your site team writes every single week:

→ Daily site reports from rough phone notes
→ Meeting minutes with a proper action table
→ First drafts of RAMS and method statements
→ COSHH assessments pulled from a safety data sheet
→ Subcontract tenders compared like for like, exclusions flagged
→ Variation letters drafted and sent the same day

None of this needs new software. ChatGPT or Claude can do all of it today.

The rule that matters: AI drafts, a competent person checks and signs off. CDM 2015 duties stay with people.

Start with one task you do every day. Add a second after a month.

I have ranked 15 use cases by time saved and how easy they are to start. Full guide is linked in the comments.

## Post 2: Hook / time-saver (attach ai-use-cases-in-construction-hook.png)

One week on a £6.5m school extension. Five small AI jobs.

Monday: revised drainage drawings summarised in 2 minutes.
Tuesday: four M&E tenders compared. The cheapest had excluded commissioning and builder's work. Worth about £38,000.
Wednesday: groundworks RAMS checked. No permit to dig, no confined space assessment for a 2.4m manhole. Sent back with three comments.
Thursday: progress meeting minutes out the same afternoon, not next Tuesday.
Friday: verbal canopy instruction confirmed in writing within hours.

Around 14 hours of typing saved. But the bigger win was what got caught.

That is what AI in construction actually looks like on a normal UK job.
