## Post 1: Value / listicle (attach best-ai-tools-for-estimating-listicle.png)

Every week someone asks me which AI tool will price their jobs.

Honest answer: none of them. AI does not know your labour rates, your suppliers or your appetite for risk.

But that is not where estimators lose their hours anyway.

On a £650k flat conversion tender we walked through, the AI stack cut 38 hours to 22. Here is where the time came from:

→ Tender pack summary: 7 hours down to 2
→ Scope gaps and tender queries: 3 hours down to 1
→ Bill structure drafted in NRM2 style: 4 hours down to 1
→ Three M&E quotes normalised into one table: 3 hours down to 1
→ Qualifications drafted for the estimator to edit

Pricing time? Unchanged. Eight hours before, eight hours after.

The biggest wins were catches, not speed: a missing fire strategy drawing and an M&E quote that quietly excluded builder's work.

Read, measure, check with AI. Price with your own numbers.

## Post 2: Hook / time-saver (attach best-ai-tools-for-estimating-hook.png)

38 hours to price a tender. 22 with the right tools.

And not one minute of the saving came from AI setting rates.

The best "AI estimating tool" is actually a small stack:

1. A general assistant (Claude or ChatGPT) to read the pack and find the gaps
2. A take-off tool to measure faster
3. Your own spreadsheet or estimating software and your own rate library

Ask a chatbot what a stud partition costs per m2 and it will give you a confident number. It might be years old, from the wrong region, or made up.

Use AI where the hours go: reading, structuring, checking.
