## Post 1: Value / listicle (attach chatgpt-prompts-for-quantity-surveyors-listicle.png)

Most QS people who try ChatGPT give up after one go.

They ask it to price a bill, it invents a set of rates, and they decide AI is useless for commercial work.

The problem is the job they gave it. AI is poor at knowing your rates. It is very good at the structure, the cross-checking and the first draft.

These are the six jobs where it earns its keep:

→ Take-off notes into NRM2 bill items
→ Tender returns into one comparison table
→ Interim applications checked against site notes
→ Variation assessments drafted from the instruction
→ Contract clauses summarised with time limits quoted exactly
→ Payment and pay less notice wording

Two phrases make every one of them safe: "Do not invent rates or quantities" and "Show every step of the arithmetic".

The QS still makes every call. The AI just does the rekeying.

## Post 2: Hook / time-saver (attach chatgpt-prompts-for-quantity-surveyors-hook.png)

A drylining sub on a 32-unit job in Nottingham applies for £412,600.

The usual routine: rekey it into your own spreadsheet, walk the site, cross-check every line against your notes and the variation register. Two and a half to three hours.

This time the QS pasted the application, last month's valuation, the site walk notes and the variation register into AI with one structured prompt.

It came back in under a minute with two flags:

Level 3 partitions claimed at 100 percent. Site notes said 70. That is £12,300.
Two variations claimed with no instruction in the register. That is £12,650.

Assessed gross £387,650. Retention, previously certified and the net due all worked out with the arithmetic shown.

The QS checked the photos, confirmed the two variations with the site manager, and sent the payment notice. About 40 minutes, start to finish.

The AI did not decide anything. It found the two lines that mattered.
